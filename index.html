Method

Building Legislative Intelligence — a transparent methodology for tracking, connecting, and explaining changes to Miami 21.

Why CodeLine Exists

Miami 21 evolves through ordinances that are introduced, revised, and adopted over time. Those changes accumulate, making it difficult to understand how today's regulations relate to previous amendments.

Official documents are public, but they are dispersed across multiple systems and are rarely connected into a single legislative history.

CodeLine Miami organizes that history. It connects ordinances over time, explains each amendment in plain language, and links every record directly to its original source.

Scope

CodeLine focuses on City of Miami legislative amendments to Miami 21. It does not track:

  • State legislation
  • County ordinances
  • Administrative decisions
  • Building permits or development applications

...unless they directly modify Miami 21 as enacted through a City ordinance.

Live Local Act

The Florida Live Local Act can supersede portions of Miami 21 for qualifying projects near transit. Because it is state legislation — not a City of Miami ordinance — it is referenced only where necessary to explain interactions with Miami 21.

It is not maintained as part of the amendment database. For any project in a transit corridor, verify current Live Local applicability against the state statute directly.
